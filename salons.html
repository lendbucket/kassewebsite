FAQ

Common questions about Kasse for Hair Salons.

Does Kasse work for booth renters and commission stylists at the same salon?
Yes. Kasse handles every employment type simultaneously. Booth renters pay rent via ACH automatically on your schedule. Commission stylists get their commission calculated from every transaction. W2 employees get full payroll with tax withholding. All under the same dashboard.
Can stylists see their own commissions without seeing other stylists' earnings?
Yes. Role-based access means each stylist sees only their own earnings, schedule, and clients. Owners and managers see everyone. Permissions are fully configurable down to individual features.
Does Kasse replace Vagaro or GlossGenius?
Yes — and Kasse includes features neither offers, like built-in payroll, Kasse Banking, commission tracking from every transaction, and profit-per-service intelligence. We also migrate your data from Vagaro, Square, or GlossGenius for free.
How does TDLR license tracking work?
Kasse verifies cosmetology licenses against the Texas TDLR database via the Socrata API. License expiry alerts fire 60 days before expiration. You're always audit-ready.
