The problems Kasse solves

What every pet grooming owner knows too well.

01
Pet info lives on index cards
Temperament notes, allergies, preferred products, vaccine expiration dates — scattered across paper cards that get lost or misfiled. Kasse stores everything in searchable digital pet profiles.
02
Parents call to ask if their pet is ready
Kasse sends automatic SMS updates as each pet moves through the grooming stages. Owners know where their pet is without calling.
03
Vaccine records expire without warning
A pet arrives without a current rabies certificate. Kasse tracks vaccine expiration dates and flags bookings when records are approaching expiry — before the appointment, not at drop-off.
Built for Pet Grooming

Everything your business needs, in one place.

Every feature is built specifically for how pet grooming operate — not adapted from a generic business tool.

🐕
Pet Profiles
Every pet has a full profile — breed, age, weight, color, temperament notes ("gets anxious with dryer — use low heat"), health conditions, vaccine records with expiry tracking, vet contact, and emergency contact. Linked to the owner's client profile.
📋
Drop-Off Board
Visual board showing every pet currently in the shop. Status: Dropped Off → Bathing → Drying → Grooming → Done → Picked Up. Groomers advance status from their tablet. Owner gets SMS at each stage automatically.
📸
Photo Updates
Groomers send mid-groom photos to owners with one tap — photo taken on the iPad, sent via SMS instantly. "Max is looking great! Almost done." Parents love it. Referrals follow.
📅
Vaccination Tracking
Store rabies, bordetella, DHPP, and custom vaccines. Expiry alerts fire 30 days before records expire. Bookings get flagged if any vaccine is expired or expiring soon. Upload vaccine PDFs directly from the booking confirmation link.
💰
Multi-Pet Checkout
Clients with multiple pets get a single checkout covering all pets seen that day. Each pet's services itemized, tips per groomer allocated, receipt sent via email or SMS.
⭐
After-Groom Review Requests
2 hours after pickup, Kasse sends an automatic review request. If the rating is 4-5 stars, client is directed to Google. Internal feedback for lower ratings. Your Google rating goes up automatically.
FAQ

Common questions about Kasse for Pet Grooming.

Can I store vaccine records as PDFs?
Yes. Vaccine records can be uploaded as PDFs or photos directly from the booking confirmation email that owners receive. They're stored in the pet's profile permanently.
How does the drop-off board work?
The drop-off board is a dedicated view in Kasse showing every pet currently in the shop with their current grooming stage. Groomers tap to advance the status on their tablet or phone. The board updates in real time on any screen.
