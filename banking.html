Features

Everything included, nothing bolted on.

Every feature is native to Kasse — built into the same platform you use for appointments, payments, and your team. No integrations to break, no separate subscriptions.

🏦
Kasse Business Checking
A real FDIC-insured business checking account. Kasse Pay settlements land here next day. ACH sends and receives. Full routing and account number for any external payment. No minimum balance, no monthly fees.
📄
Bill Pay & Vendor Management
Add vendors — rent, supplies, utilities, subscriptions. Schedule one-time or recurring ACH payments. Bill calendar shows upcoming payments with 3-day advance alerts. Never miss a payment or pay a late fee again.
📊
Profit & Loss Reports
Monthly P&L combining Kasse Pay revenue, expenses paid through bill pay, and payroll. Revenue, COGS, operating expenses, gross profit, net profit. Compare to prior month or prior year. Export to PDF or CSV.
🔗
QuickBooks Online Sync
Two-way sync with QuickBooks Online. Every transaction categorized and synced. Your accountant works in QBO — Kasse keeps it updated automatically. No manual exports, no duplicate entry.
💳
Kasse Business Debit Card
Physical and virtual Kasse debit card connected to your Kasse Banking balance. Real-time transaction notifications. Spending controls. Interchange revenue returned to Kasse — keeping your platform costs low.
📈
Cash Flow Intelligence
See your next 30 days of projected cash flow — settlements coming in, bills going out, payroll due. Know before you're short. Kasse alerts you when your balance approaches a threshold you set.
