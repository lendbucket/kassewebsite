FAQ

Common questions about Kasse for nail salons.

Can we run walk-ins and appointments at the same time?
Yes — that's exactly what Kasse is designed for. Walk-ins join a digital queue, appointment clients book into the calendar, and both show in the same live view for techs and the front desk. No double-booking, no confusion about who was next.
How does the nail art gallery work for attracting new clients?
Each tech has a portfolio gallery inside Kasse with a shareable public link. Clients can browse the gallery, see specific styles they like, and request that tech at booking. It functions like a built-in Instagram portfolio that converts to actual appointments.
Does Kasse handle commission techs and booth renters at the same nail salon?
Yes. Each tech or booth renter has their own pay structure configured. Commission techs get their earnings calculated from every transaction. Booth renters get auto-debited via ACH on your schedule. Both in the same payroll dashboard.
