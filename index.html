Payroll & HCM

Every type of employee, handled automatically.

W2 employees. 1099 commission stylists. Booth renters. Hybrid models. Kasse calculates commissions from every transaction, handles tax withholding, collects booth rent via ACH, and disburses payroll via Wise.

  • Commission auto-calculated from every transaction — flat %, tiered, or per-service rates
  • W2 tax withholding calculated automatically — federal, state, FICA
  • Booth rent collected via ACH on your schedule — no chasing, no manual invoicing
  • Direct deposit via Wise API — staff paid on time every pay period, automatically
  • Kasse Tax Service: quarterly 941, year-end W2 and 1099-NEC filing ($49/mo addon)
  • Staff see their own real-time earnings — no more "how much do I make this week?" questions
