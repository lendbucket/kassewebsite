Payroll & HCM

Every type of employee, handled automatically.

W2 employees. 1099 commission stylists. Booth renters. Hybrid models. Kasse calculates commissions, tracks hours, deducts booth rent via ACH, handles tax withholding, and disburses via Wise — all in one place.

  • Commission auto-calculated per transaction — flat %, tiered, or per-service
  • W2 tax withholding (federal + state + FICA) calculated automatically
  • Booth rent collected via ACH on your schedule — no chasing
  • Direct deposit via Wise API — staff get paid on time, automatically
  • Kasse Tax Service: quarterly 941, year-end W2/1099-NEC filing ($49/mo addon)
  • Staff see their own earnings in real time — no more "how much do I make?" questions
