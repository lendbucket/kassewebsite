The problems Kasse solves

What every barbershop owner knows too well.

01
The clipboard queue is chaos
Paper lists, crossed-out names, arguments about who was next. Clients leave when they can't see their wait time. Kasse's digital queue shows every client their real-time position and texts them when they're up.
02
Chasing booth rent every week
Booth renters pay late, forget, or short-pay. You have to ask, remind, and sometimes confront. Kasse auto-debits booth rent via ACH on your schedule. You get paid first, every time.
03
Walk-ins don't want appointments
Barbershops run on walk-ins. Forcing clients into an appointment-first system kills the culture. Kasse is built hybrid — walk-ins join a digital queue, regulars can book ahead, both show on the same calendar.

FAQ

Common questions about Kasse for Barbershops.

Can clients join the queue from outside the shop?
Yes. Clients can scan a QR code at the door, use a link from your website or Instagram bio, or click through from your Google Business Profile. They join the digital queue and get SMS updates.
Does Kasse work if we're mostly walk-in with some appointments?
That's exactly what Kasse is designed for. Walk-ins join the queue, appointments slot into the calendar alongside the queue, and barbers see everything in one view. No separate systems.
How does the TV board work?
Kasse has a dedicated TV board display mode — a full-screen view designed to run on a TV or monitor at the front of the shop. It shows current client in chair, next up, and the full queue. Barbers update it from their phone as they complete cuts.
