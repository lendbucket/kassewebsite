FAQ

Common questions about Kasse for Gyms & Fitness Studios.

What check-in methods does Kasse support?
QR code (from the Kasse client app), key fob (NFC tap), and PIN entry on a keypad. You can mix and match — some members use QR, others prefer fob. Each logs a timestamped check-in record.
How does Kasse handle expired or failed membership payments?
Kasse automatically retries failed payments after 3 days. If still failed, it sends the member a secure payment update link via SMS and email. After 7 days without resolution, membership is suspended and access revoked. Owner gets alerts at each stage.
Can personal trainers see their client history and session logs?
Yes. Each trainer has their own view of their clients with full session history, goals, progress measurements, and AI-generated program suggestions. Clients can optionally share their progress with the trainer between sessions.
